Title: Zoho Books Credit Note: Refund or Apply to Invoice? #Shorts

Description:
A customer returns goods in Zoho Books: you create one credit note, then the path depends on the invoice. Open the invoice, More, Create Credit Note, and enter the exact returned quantity. Unpaid invoice: the credit settles it, the invoice becomes Paid or Partially Paid and the credit note becomes Closed. Paid invoice: go to Sales, Credit Notes, select the note, More, Refund, enter the amount and pick the cash or bank account.
